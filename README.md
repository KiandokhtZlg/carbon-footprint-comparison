# carbon-footprint-comparison


This notebook compares the lifetime carbon emissions of solar panels and coal energy based on energy production per m².

## Methods
- Solar irradiation: 2000 kWh/m²/year
- Efficiency: 14%
- Performance ratio: 0.80
- Lifetime: 30 years

## Results
- Solar CO2 emissions: 268.8 kg
- Coal CO2 emissions: 6720 kg
